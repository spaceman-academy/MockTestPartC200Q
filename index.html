Navigating and answering

  • All questions are Part C type and appear in mixed order, as in the real exam. Click a question number in the palette to go to that question.
  • Each question has four options numbered (1) to (4). Exactly one is correct.
  • Your answer is saved only when you click Save & Next or Save & Mark for Review. If you choose an option and move to another question with Back, Next or the palette, that option is not saved.
  • Save & Next saves your answer and goes to the next question.
  • Save & Mark for Review saves your answer, marks the question for review and goes to the next question.
  • Mark for Review & Next marks the question for review and goes to the next question. If an option is chosen, it is saved too.
  • Clear Response removes the chosen option.
  • Back and Next move between questions without saving.
  • You can submit only after 3 hours from the start of the test. When the timer reaches zero, the test submits itself.
  • If the page reloads, your saved answers and the timer are restored automatically.
